Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Invictus Tournament Maker ("the App") is designed with your privacy in mind. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our application.

1. Data Stored Locally

The tournament data you create within the App, including tournaments, teams, players, brackets, matches, and related information, is stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers unless you choose to publish a dataset to the Community Dataset Market (see Section 5).

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements, in accordance with Google's privacy policies and applicable laws.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your device settings, consent choices, and applicable regulations.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

3. Analytics

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how users interact with the App and to improve its performance, stability, and features.

Analytics may collect information such as app usage events, device information, app version, approximate location, and diagnostic information. Analytics data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

We do not use analytics to directly identify individual users.

4. Optional Sign-In

The App works fully without an account. You only need to sign in with Google if you want to publish a dataset to the Community Dataset Market or report a dataset.

When you sign in, Firebase Authentication receives your name, email address, and a unique user ID from Google. Your email address is used only to sign you in; it is not shown to other users and is not stored in the Community Dataset Market database. Your display name and user ID are stored with the datasets you publish and the reports you send.

5. Community Dataset Market

Browsing and downloading community datasets does not require an account. A dataset contains team names, colours, strength values, flags, and a title and description written by its author.

When you publish a dataset, it is uploaded to Cloud Firestore and is public : anyone using the App can see and download it, together with your display name and the date it was published. Please do not include personal information or content that infringes someone else's rights. We may remove datasets that violate these rules or that are reported by other users.

If you report a dataset, we store the dataset ID, your user ID, the reason you selected, and the time, in order to review the content. Download counts are stored per dataset and are not linked to individual users.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account and every dataset you have published at any time:

In the App: Settings → Sample Data → Community datasets → Delete account.
On the web: https://invictus-tournament-maker.web.app/delete-account
Or by email: orazchollaev@gmail.com

Deleting your account removes your published datasets from the Community Dataset Market. Copies that other users have already downloaded remain on their own devices. Reports you have sent may be kept for moderation purposes.

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions that are necessary for certain features to function properly. These permissions are used only for their intended functionality.

8. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services provided by Google, including Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Authentication, and Cloud Firestore. These services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at Google Privacy & Terms .

9. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party services used by the App, including advertising and analytics services, may have their own requirements and age-related policies. Signing in and publishing to the Community Dataset Market are not intended for children under 13.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: orazchollaev@gmail.com
